Worthy Travel Spots


Paris, France

Paris is the capital of France and one of the most visited cities in the world. It is known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture.

I recommend Paris for its iconic landmarks, charming neighborhoods, and rich history. It is a great place for capturing aesthetic Instagram photos, especially during golden hour and around the Salcedo Weekend Market.


New York City, USA

New York City is the most populous city in the United States and a global hub for finance, culture, and entertainment.

I recommend New York City for its iconic landmarks, diverse neighborhoods, and vibrant arts scene. It is a great place for capturing aesthetic Instagram photos, especially during golden hour and around the famous Times Square.


I recommend New York City for its iconic landmarks, vibrant streets, and unforgettable city views. It is a great destination for travel photos, especially street shots, skyline views, famous attractions, and evening pictures.


Nachi Falls, Japan

Nachi Falls is one of the most famous waterfalls in Japan, located in the Kii Peninsula. It is known for its stunning natural beauty and spiritual significance.

I recommend Nachi Falls for its breathtaking scenery and serene atmosphere. It is a great place for capturing aesthetic Instagram photos, especially during golden hour and around the surrounding temples.
