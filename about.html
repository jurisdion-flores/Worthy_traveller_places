About Juris

I’m Juris, a travel enthusiast who loves discovering beautiful places and sharing memorable destinations worth visiting and exploring.

Worthy Traveller Places is a personal guide for people looking for beautiful and memorable destinations around Metro Manila. I share places with interesting architecture, relaxing cafes, scenic streets, and unique My spaces worth visiting and exploring. My goal is to help you discover new places, plan your next adventure, and find inspiration for your next travel destination.
